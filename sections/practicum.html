About the Company

AF Payments Inc. is a Philippine company specializing in electronic payment solutions and automated fare collection systems for public transportation. It operates the beep™ stored-value card and mobile application, enabling secure and convenient cashless payments across rail systems and selected public transport services.

The company supports smarter transportation through digital payment technologies, efficient fare collection, and data-driven solutions that improve operational efficiency and commuter experience.
